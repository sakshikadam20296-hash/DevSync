💡 About DevSync

A smarter way to manage work.

DevSync is designed to help organizations understand how employees spend their working time and how efficiently daily tasks are completed.

Employees can track their work sessions, manage tasks, record daily activities and monitor their productivity.

Managers get a centralized view of employee performance and productivity, helping them make better decisions using work data.
